Ex-Dividend & Dividend-Capture Model

Empirical study of price behaviour around ex-dividend dates for Hyperliquid equity perps and their underlyings. Buy the day before ex, sell on the ex-date.

notional $100,000 · WHT 15% · exit ex_open · fills maker · perp fee 3.0bps/side

What the data says

  • Across 16 ex-dividend events, the underlying moved on average -31.85× the dividend across the ex-date — nowhere near the textbook 1.00×. The dividend is a rounding error next to the day-to-day move.
  • On the 8 events with Hyperliquid perp coverage, the perp moved -17.09× the dividend, while overnight it wandered up to 46.2× the dividend away from the entry.
  • The delta-neutral play (B) averaged $296 per $100,000 event — but that number is dominated by basis noise, not by the ~0.1–0.2% dividend edge.
  • Conclusion: the dividend signal (~0.1–0.2%) is an order of magnitude smaller than the overnight basis noise (~0.5–1%+). The edge is real but tiny; the variance is not. Only worth it with maker fills, a short hold, and many repetitions — exactly as suspected.
